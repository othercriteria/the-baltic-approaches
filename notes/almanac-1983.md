## Key narrative dates, cross-checked

- 21 Oct (Fri): ENDEX. Full moon that night — the reception at
  the mess under a full moon, for what it's worth.
- 6 Nov (Sun): M-day. Waxing crescent 5% — mobilization's first
  nights are effectively moonless. ✓ favorable to the quiet list.
- 8 Nov (Tue) 0351: first contact. Crescent 17%, sets early
  evening — the attack goes in on a dark night. ✓ text.
- 13 Nov (Sun): the narrows. Wax gibbous 67% — but the day was
  fought under a 200 m ceiling; moonlight moot. ✓ no conflict.
- 18–20 Nov: FULL MOON over the mid-war pause/harassment nights.
  Available texture: the harassment gunner under a hunter's moon.
- 24–26 Nov (red's assembly nights): waning gibbous → last
  quarter; moonrise ~2200–2330 — the EVENING assembly hours are
  genuinely dark. ✓ ch. 15's corrected line ("does not rise until
  near midnight... his assembly hours are dark ones").
- 27 Nov (Sun) 0340: red's preparation. Last quarter 41%, risen
  since ~2330 — pre-dawn is moonlit IF clear; the text's black
  sleet (overcast) carries it. ✓ but any clear-sky rewrite of
  that night must remember the half-moon.
- 28 Nov (Mon) 0500: counterstroke H-hour. Civil twilight 0733,
  sunrise 0815. **Ch. 18's stated 0742 corrected to 0733 this
  date** (seam of dark-work/day-work ≈ 0735–0740).
- 6 Dec (Tue) 2400: cease-fire. New-moon dark, the year's nearly
  shortest days. The last adjustments crawl in real darkness.

## Holidays and observances in the window

FRG (Schleswig-Holstein, 1983 law — Protestant north):
- Sun 13 Nov: **Volkstrauertag** — national day of mourning for
  the war dead. Day 6 of the fictional war. (Unused texture of
  the first rank.)
- Wed 16 Nov: **Buß- und Bettag** (Day of Repentance and Prayer)
  — then still a PUBLIC HOLIDAY in the FRG.
- Sun 20 Nov: **Totensonntag** — Protestant remembrance of the
  dead; churches read the year's death roll.
- Sun 27 Nov: **First Advent.** Red's great effort opens at 0340
  on Advent Sunday; the counterstroke follows on the Monday.
- Fri 11 Nov: St. Martin's Day — northern children's lantern
  processions (Laternelaufen); in the evacuated districts, the
  lanterns don't walk this year.

Denmark:
- No November public holidays. Thu 10 Nov: Mortensaften (roast
  goose — cultural, kept in homes). Tue 6 Dec: St. Nicholas
  (minor) — the cease-fire lands on his night. Tue 13 Dec: Lucia.

## Tides and sea state

- **Baltic (the corps' right flank, the Schlei, the Belts):
  effectively tideless** — astronomical tide 10–20 cm; what moves
  the water is wind set-up (storm surge/Sturmhochwasser), which
  is weather, not schedule. FLAG: the draft twice uses "one tide"
  in Baltic-flank contexts (ch. 16, the raid "reinforced, one
  tide"; ch. 19, Vestergaard's "one tide from ours"). As
  soldier's idiom for ~six hours it may pass; as literal
  constraint it is wrong for that coast. DK to rule: keep as
  idiom or recast ("one night," "one dark").
- **North Sea coast (Husum, the Eider mouth): real semidiurnal
  tides, ~3 m range.** But the lower Eider has been controlled by
  the **Eidersperrwerk (completed 1973)** — Friedrichstadt/
  Nordfeld crossing operations are NOT tide-governed in 1983.
  Do not invent tide constraints the barrage removed. (Ferry and
  barrage interplay: barrier normally open, closed against
  surge.)
- Great Belt ferries: schedule-governed, weather-limited (gales),
  not tide-limited to any narrative-relevant degree.
