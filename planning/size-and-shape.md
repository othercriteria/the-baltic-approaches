## 1. The arithmetic that frames everything

The Goal is ~100–110k words: 40 chapters, ~14 concept stations,
~5–6 months of story time, a mentor contacted ~10 times, and an
11-chapter generalization movement at the end. **Our target is half
that canvas** holding a 3-week war plus whatever pre-war we buy.
So the transposition is not a scaling-down of every element by half
— some elements (the ensemble, the front, the B-plot) cost nearly
the same at any length, so the cuts concentrate in the concept
ladder and the generalization movement. The White Buffalo lesson
cuts both ways here: 31k happened because the story was a 31k
story; the check below is that the transposed form honestly FILLS
55k (no padding) and honestly FITS in it (no cramming).

## 2. What the form irreducibly needs (from the structure map)

- **~7–9 concept stations** (The Goal's 14, pruned to the
  operational-art core). Candidate ladder: misleading attrition
  metrics believed → what is the goal of this defense → dependent
  events + fluctuations (march tables; the 90-hour clock is the
  hike) → the constraint identified (what actually meters the
  corps' tempo) → exploit/subordinate (air allocation, reserve
  release, the screen) → the enemy's constraint (red's
  railhead/consumption; the G-2 turn — reading the opponent's
  Herbie) → the window (the counterstroke as throughput event) →
  meta-station (whose consumption model is the argument standing
  on — the instrument's META-claim as the staff's earned
  epistemology).
- **Each station = one derivation scene + operational consequence**
  (the map's law: payload lands only where an opponent forces it,
  a failure teaches it, or a physical/staged model enacts it).
- **~6–8 Jonah contacts**, rationed, each with a homework gate;
  the exit enacted as doctrine before the climax.
- **Valve chapters** (B-plot/human-scale, 1–1.5k words) after
  every dense chapter; B-plot ~15–20% of wordcount, thematically
  rhymed (what is being defended, at what rate of expenditure),
  retired before the didactic climax.
- **One earned NBC scene** (locked, nbc-conceit.md), red's week-3
  staff-argument as its red half.
- **Ensemble establishment** for a binational staff — costlier
  than The Goal's (nationality, language, two capitals), budget it.

## 3. Chapter/word discipline (WB undershoot lesson, applied)

Per-unit floors, so thinness is diagnosable: seminar/derivation
chapters 2.2–3k; set-piece chapters (exercise endex, the window)
3–3.5k; valve chapters 1–1.5k; The Goal's long/short alternation
kept; never two derivation chapters adjacent. At these grains 55k
= **~20 chapters** (18–22 band).

## 4. The shape options, forced

**A. Three parts with a pre-war overture (lean 20-ch, ~55k).**
- *Part I — The Exercise* (~5 ch, ~13k): the autumn exercise
  (BOLD GUARD-shaped) as the plant-before-the-crisis: the settled
  metrics believed, the staff rehearsing under the OLD commander,
  Jonah introduced, the change of command, war origin gathering
  under exercise cover (the November criterion working for us).
  The exercise is the sanctioned iteration laboratory (the
  Critical Chain classroom move, period-native as TEWT/CPX) and
  the baseline every wartime deviation is measured against.
- *Part II — The Covering Force* (~9 ch, ~25k): W-hour and the
  90-hour clock (the hike, walkable); the screen; metrics collide
  with ground truth; the constraint found; the air-allocation
  iterations; hold-line politics; one division liaison window and
  one Karup window (the retained bake-off sourcebooks, spent
  sparingly).
- *Part III — The Window* (~6 ch, ~17k): the envelope act break
  (day 13–14, wargame-stable — the door holds, the knocking gets
  louder); the window recognized, argued, and taken; victory
  scoped to the corps' reach; epistemic ending (the meta-station),
  the war ending around the corps, not because of it.
- *Case for:* the exercise part solves iteration honestly, gives
  the reader the baseline that makes wartime confusion legible
  (the reader-ahead mechanism needs a known normal), and carries
  the change-of-command device natively. *Case against:* ~13k
  spent before the war; the opening must carry jeopardy without
  combat (The Goal proves a pre-crisis opening can hold — ch. 1
  is an ultimatum, not a battle — ours is a commander who missed
  the rehearsal).

**B. Cold open at W-hour, exercise in flashback (~20 ch).**
The Goal's own move (the cigar pivot): open in crisis, reach the
exercise through memory. Buys ~8–10k for war texture; costs the
laboratory its own present-tense stakes and makes the iteration
engine lean entirely on the in-war cadence (daily conference,
estimate-vs-outcome log). Genre-fresher opening; harder didactic
plumbing. Viable if Phase-1 outlining shows Part I sagging.

**C. The 60k upper bound with interleaf windows.**
As A, plus recurring division-commander and Karup-conference
chapters (the declined outlines as minor-POV interleaves rather
than liaison visits only). Delivers the ruling's
"visibility above and below" most fully; risks ensemble bloat and
the cramming failure mode; only if Phase-1 sketches show the corps
spine underfilling. A red-side POV thread (Red Army-style) is
explicitly NOT budgeted at this length — red is read through the
G-2, which is the book's argument anyway.

**Lean (recorded, not ruled): A**, with B held as the demotion
path if Part I fails in sketch, and C's interleaves available as
insurance against underrun. This matches the premise's original
claim — the didactic form carries length honestly; 20 chapters
each with a load-bearing payload is the honest 55k.

## 4a. The 45k and 65k variants — what the sizing costs us, marginally

*(Commissioned by DK 2026-07-23: price the increments around 55k,
don't just describe the point.)*

**The cost model.** Split the budget into fixed and variable. The
roughly length-invariant fixtures: ensemble establishment for a
binational staff (~8–10k across the early chapters), a B-plot with
enough autonomy to clear the Phoenix/Paige failure bar (~7–8k),
the NBC scene (~2.5k), and the window/climax sequence (~5k) —
call it **~23–25k fixed**. What remains buys concept stations at
**~3.5–4.5k per station all-in** (derivation scene + operational
consequence + its share of valve rhythm, at the §3 floors). At 55k
that is ~30k variable ≈ the 8-station ladder with ~2 iteration
cycles each — which is why 55k is the planning point.

**45k (−10k): the marginal cost is structural, and it compounds.**
Variable drops to ~20–22k ≈ 5–6 stations. The −10k must come out
of one of three places, all load-bearing:
1. *Stations.* Cutting to 6 means losing two of: the G-2 turn
   (the enemy's constraint — the most original payload in the
   book), the meta-station (the META-claim epistemology — the
   instrument's actual deliverable), or the exploit/subordinate
   pair (the ToC core). There is no station whose loss is cheap.
2. *The laboratory.* Part I dies; the shape forcibly demotes to
   option B (cold open, exercise as flashback), the iteration
   engine loses its sanctioned-rehearsal leg, and the
   commander-who-missed-the-exercise device loses its on-page
   referent.
3. *Staging room.* Keeping 8 stations at ~2.7k all-in means
   derivations without consequences — payload narrated, not
   earned. That is PowerPoint-in-prose by construction, and the
   reader-ahead mechanism (goal-read-notes §2) is the first
   casualty: legibility costs words.
The annex CANNOT rescue this (§0 boundary rule: apparatus carries
reference, not derivation). Note also 45k ≈ 1.45× White Buffalo —
half the deliberate craft increment the premise was chosen to
deliver. **Verdict: 45k is not a trimmed version of this book; it
is a different, lesser book** (a war procedural with didactic
seasoning — The Goal's Part II without its plant-before or its
method-after). It should not be held as the overrun fallback;
overrun pressure is answered by cutting §5-tier features, not by
re-targeting.

**65k (+10k): the marginal purchase is optional delivery of things
already ruled desirable; the marginal cost is discipline.**
Best uses of the increment, in descending value:
1. *The interleaves* (~3–4 chapters, ~9k): division-commander and
   Karup-conference minor-POV chapters — full delivery of the
   bake-off ruling's design directive ("visibility above and
   below without their interiority" becomes visibility WITH
   carefully-rationed interiority), spending the two declined
   outlines as the pre-built sourcebooks they are. Highest value
   because it buys an existing DK directive with existing assets.
2. *A ninth station + a third iteration cycle*: the
   MOPP/protective-posture tempo tax (nbc-conceit residual (a)
   already wants it on-screen) or the Danish-LOC/CAL-3 logistics
   station; and the air-question whipsaw honestly deserves a
   third cycle (its four-swing history IS the meta-claim).
3. *B-plot autonomy*: a genuinely independent domestic arc at
   WB-interiority standard rather than a rhymed accompaniment.
Costs: the padding/cramming risk swaps sides (The Goal's own
late-book generalization bloat is the cautionary shape); the
undershoot exposure grows with unit count (24–26 units each
quietly 15% thin = ~9k missing — a "65k" plan lands at 56k unless
the per-unit floors are policed hard); and drafting/critique cost
scales superlinearly with units. One external datum cuts the other
way: 65k narrative + apparatus reads at trade-military-fiction
norm (80–120k books), so 65k is the more genre-normal object —
but the increment discipline (WB 31k → deliberate next step) was
the stated reason for 50–60k, and 65k leaks past it.

**The asymmetry, stated.** Going down 10k costs structure — the
stations, the laboratory, and the staging room that make the form
work; the cost curve is convex below ~50k. Going up 10k buys
optional delivery of already-ruled directives at a manageable
discipline cost; the curve is roughly linear above, until bloat.
**Therefore: plan at 55k; let 60–65k be the permitted overflow
direction iff Phase-1 outlining shows the interleaves earning
their words scene by scene; treat 45k as out of scope absent an
Andon conversation** (if outlining reveals the story wanting to be
45k, that is the CLAUDE.md natural-size clause firing, not a
trim). The narrative-only ruling (§0) additionally hands every
variant ~2–4k of relief — orientation and restatement move to
apparatus — which should be spent on staging, not pocketed as
extra stations.

## 5. What is deliberately cut at this length (named, per process)

  epistemology, not a management-theory coda.

  phone-tier contact rhythm instead).
- The Zealand feint as an on-page thread (stays what campaign 2

- Any red-interiority chapters; unconfirmed other-theater
  chemical reports stay G-2-summary ambience (nbc-conceit).
- The promotion frame (The Goal's scope-escalation engine) — our
  scope escalation is the staff-section-walls arc (G-3 discovers
  he needs G-2/G-4), which is cheaper and already the ruled arc.

## 6. Register lean (input to the CLAUDE.md decision, not a ruling)

Seminar-overt, held — conditional on engineering the
**reader-ahead mechanism** (goal-read-notes §2): the seminar reads
as the staff catching up to the reader, never as the author
briefing them. The wargame instrument is the tool that makes this
engineerable (we can compute what the staged situation lets a
reader infer). Where the reader cannot plausibly run ahead, the
payload must be re-staged (failure-taught or adversarially forced),
not lectured. Phoenix is the cautionary bound: name-the-source
legibility is forbidden by restraint rules, so our version of
overt is The Goal's (unapologetic derivation in-scene), never
Kim's (citation in-scene).
