## 1. Decisions made in this outline (delegated; argued here)

### 1.1 Q3 — F7's antagonist: the theater's own endorsement

The audit-fight in ch. 20 is against **COMBALTAP's assessment
cell at Karup — concretely the theater plans/assessment chief who
adopted the corps' confession wall as a "theater instrument" in
ch. 15.** His campaign report needs the corps to have been RIGHT,
because his own mid-war endorsement is on the line: he
institutionalized the method while it was still an open question,
and a report that says "the corps was solvent, not right" indicts
his adoption, not just the corps' luck. Copenhagen's appetite for
a Danish success story (the new Danish COMLANDJUT's victory,
three weeks into the alternation) is the ambient pressure behind
him, kept ambient — no minister on stage.

Why this candidate over the sheet's alternatives: (a) it is the
allocation sheet's own lean ("the corps' own success-hungry
higher echelon") given a face the book has already met — the
Karup conference device (chs. 12, 15) plants him twice before he
turns; (b) his motive is self-defense of a good-faith act, not
venality — per the reader-contract's generosity about comrades
and rule 2.8 against tidy villains; (c) it re-teaches the
meta-station one turn deeper: an instrument becomes doctrine the
moment it succeeds once, and the people who laminate it are the
ones it saved. F7's fight is thus structurally the SAME event as
S1: a flattering metric (victory) believed by earnest men — the
book ends on its first lesson recurring one echelon up.

Working identity: **Oberst Søren Bjelke (DA), COMBALTAP
assessments** — name provisional like all names. He appears
voice-only at the Karup conferences (chs. 12, 15 — the adopting
voice), and in person only in ch. 20. Per the perimeter rules he
is never interior; per rule 5 he IS permitted aphorisms — he is
what the drafting voice sounds like when it is wrong, which is
the tic weaponized in-fiction.
