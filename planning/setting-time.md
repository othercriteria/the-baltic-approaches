# Setting time: year and season

*Opened 2026-07-20 on DK's ruling that "1986" was an arbitrary
placeholder, now unpinned. The repo name and working title keep the
number until a real title exists; nothing narrative is committed to
it.*

## The year (unpinned; re-pick in Phase 1)

Criteria for the re-pick, in rough priority order:

1. **Doctrine-arc position.** The reader contract wants a periphery
   still running legacy doctrine while the center reforms. AirLand
   Battle publishes August 1982 (FM 100-5), revises 1986; the Soviet
   counter-reaction (Ogarkov's writings, OMG maturation) runs
   1982-86. The window where "published but not yet arrived where
   the protagonist is" rings true is roughly **1983-1987** —
   earlier, and there's nothing to have missed; later, and the
   periphery's ignorance strains belief.
2. **Political-texture options.** Early window (1983-84): maximum
   tension — Able Archer, KAL 007, the freeze on dialogue; war
   origins are easy and grim. Late window (1986-87): Gorbachev-era
   thaw — war origins need more contrivance but gain irony (the
   collapse *into* war of a warming world), and the Soviet military's
   own reform anxiety becomes usable texture.
3. **Hardware watershed lines.** Each candidate year moves kit in
   and out of reach on both sides (e.g., what's fielded vs.
   prototype: SS-23, MT-LB variants, Leopard 2 batches, Tornado IDS
   saturation, F-16 blocks; for LANDJUT specifically, Danish
   Leopard 1A3s and drakens vs. what the WP's Baltic-facing armies
   actually held by year). The order-of-battle ledger should be
   built year-parameterized so candidate years can be diffed.
4. **Season interaction** (below): the year choice must contain a
   plausible mobilization-to-war sequence in the chosen season.
5. **Command-nationality coupling (LANDJUT).** COMLANDJUT alternated
   German/Danish (GE 1979-82, DA 1982-85, GE 1985-87 —
   reference/landjut-front.md): the year choice sets whether the
   protagonist's corps commander is German or Danish, which
   interacts with the protagonist-nationality decision
   (planning/protagonist-echelon.md, open item 1).

## The season (provisional pick: late autumn — November)

Picked provisionally to see how it feels and lands (DK's framing);
test in Phase-1 wargame sketches and sample scenes before canon.

The case for November, at Baltic latitudes (54-56°N):

- **Exercise season as war cover.** The autumn exercise cycle on
  both sides (REFORGER/Autumn Forge; WP autumn maneuvers) is the
  classic window where mobilization hides inside routine — war
  origins get texture for free, and the opening confusion clause of
  the reader contract is environmental fact before it's plot.
- **Long nights are payload.** Eight hours of daylight bends
  everything doctrinal we care about: sortie generation collapses
  into contested darkness, recon-strike degrades, movement windows
  invert. The metrics-mislead engine gets a physical accomplice.
- **The ground changes mid-campaign.** November holds the mud-to-
  frost transition: a campaign that begins road-bound in saturated
  lowland can end with frozen ground opening cross-country maneuver
  — the constraint *moves* mid-book, which is a Theory of
  Constraints gift (the bottleneck you fixed stops being the
  bottleneck).
- **The sea has a closing window.** Baltic sea state worsens through
  autumn; the WP amphibious threat against the Danish islands and
  Jutland coast wanes by December. The *enemy* is on a clock — their
  tempo pressure is legible and motivated, and the protagonist can
  learn to read an opponent's constraint (the doctrinal payload from
  the other side).
- **Fog, drizzle, low ceilings** — the Baltic November palette suits
  the register: gray war, gray light, no heroic weather.

Counter-briefs to keep live (per process rules):

- **Deep winter (Jan-Feb):** even longer nights; ice in the Belts
  and the Sound could gate or enable amphibious/over-ice movement
  (historically resonant — the 1658 March Across the Belts is the
  region's founding military ghost story); harsher survival texture
  (WB's certified strength). Costs: exercise-season war-origin cover
  is gone; ice reliability varies by year (an ice year would
  constrain the year re-pick).
- **Summer (the genre default — RSR, Team Yankee):** maximum
  daylight and sortie rates, campaign season, dust and burning
  fields. Costs: the payloads lose their environmental accomplices;
  we'd share weather with the whole genre shelf.

## Ripples of unpinning the year

- The shelf's period-line discipline now reads "≤ setting year"
  (reference/shelf.md): sources characters can know must predate the
  *chosen* year, so distillations should tag source dates, not
  assume 1986.
- CLAUDE.md's voice criterion ("nothing post-1986") inherits the
  same parameterization; updated to point here.
- The OOB ledger and any wargaming data files should carry
  in-service dates so the year re-pick is a query, not a rebuild.
