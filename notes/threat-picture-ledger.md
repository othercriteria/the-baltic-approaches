# Threat-picture ledger — what the corps knows, when

*Instituted 2026-07-24 on DK's read-finding (dk-read-log batch 2,
item 2): the understated slide into war in ch. 2 is intentional,
and the discipline behind it is this ledger — the corps'
information state about red, dated, whether or not a given item
ever lands in the text. Drafting law: no character may act on an
item before its date; the reader may know more (via red beats)
but blue may not. Extend as drafting/revision touches new
chapters; this opening version covers Part I.*

## The peacetime baseline (before October 1983)

- Twenty years of doctrine study: norms (10 km/day conventional
  variant), echelon theory (five-to-seven divisions expected on
  the LANDJUT frontage), the quick/nuclear variant's existence,
  amphibious capability against the isles. Source: doctrine
  publications, captured-document scholarship, alliance
  intelligence. This is the lens EVERYTHING early is read
  through — the confirmation-bias engine of chs. 4 and 11.
- The corps holds red's transport geography cold (rail lines
  Bad Kleinen/Herrnburg, port complexes) — targeting folders
  maintained for years. It holds nothing current on red's
  STOCKS or actual readiness states.

## The autumn's accumulation (what Hvidt inherits, 25 Oct)

- Since August: GDR railway surge east of Rostock, week over
  week. Graded: consistent with autumn maneuver season.
- September: the airliner shootdown; alliance-wide tension is
  ambient context, not corps intelligence.
- October: enemy autumn maneuvers extended twice; amphibious
  rehearsals off Rügen ended WITHOUT dispersal of shipping;
  Polish coastal exercise extended. Graded at month's end:
  "an exercise no longer shaped like an exercise" (Aakjær's
  2 Nov formulation, ch. 2).
- Hvidt's personal state at handover: three years of Defense
  Command threat assessments (Copenhagen altitude — political
  warning indicators, mobilization economics), NO current
  tactical picture ("watched BOLD LANCER from three hundred
  kilometers and a desk").

## The escalation week (2–8 Nov; ch. 2's compressed montage)

- 2 Nov (Aakjær's indicator brief): Sassnitz ferry schedules
  decoupled from trade; Rostock Bezirk hospitals deferring
  civilian admissions (unambiguous mobilization measure — the
  single hardest indicator the corps gets before contact);
  Northern Fleet sailed Friday (31 Oct). Assessment: innocent
  readings now cost more assumptions than hostile ones.
- 4 Nov: Danish government recalls key personnel (political
  tier acting on national intelligence the corps does NOT see
  in detail — the corps infers the capitals' picture from
  their actions, a deliberate asymmetry kept through the
  book).
- 5 Nov: German territorial Phase One route measures.
- 6 Nov 0640: BALTAP WARNING ORDER; 0955Z NATO alert measure.
  Content: warning, not enemy detail — the corps still holds
  NO order of battle for what is actually coming, only the
  doctrine-derived expectation.
- What the corps does NOT know at H-hour (8 Nov 0351): red's
  actual architecture (one continuous column, not echeloned
  waves — not corrected until ch. 14, day 15); red's
  objective (the port/sea-window logic — not derived until
  ch. 13); red's stocks (never known; approximated by
  audit-of-inference from ch. 14 on, and wrong at the crest
  by the margin ch. 19 pays for).

## Standing asymmetries (design law, from the outline)

- Red beats give the READER red's true state; blue never
  receives it. Every blue estimate must be derivable from
  blue's own listed sources: intercept (Stadum troop, the
  Törsbøl DF baseline), air photography (weather-gated),
  prisoners (from day 2), the enemy's observable economy
  (fires, careful targets, movement rates), and doctrine.
- The capitals know things the corps doesn't (political
  channels), and act on them without explanation — felt as
  weather (the guidance lines, the recalls), never briefed.
- Nothing NBC-specific enters blue's picture beyond ch. 6's
  behavioral reading (careful fires, bundled doctrine) — the
  conceit's information discipline.
