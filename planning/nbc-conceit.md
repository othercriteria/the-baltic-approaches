# The NBC conceit — mapping the elephant, then locking the door

*Opened 2026-07-22 (session 3845eb93) on DK's directive: map the
genre's tropes for the nuclear (plus chemical/biological) problem,
consider a novel option, and lock a pick so the book stops
lampshading. DK's constraint, verbatim intent: the genre relies on
a non-nuclear conceit ("I can't imagine things stopping at tactical
scale once that barrier is broken"); non-conventional need not be
ruled out of red's and blue's decision trees, "but it should stay
an option not exercised in this book (barring a very good argument
on the contrary)."*

## 1. Why this is now unavoidable (what the shelf documents)

The research made the elephant load-bearing, not decorative:

- **Red's default track is nuclear-first.** The 1961 directive
  allocates 93 front missiles (3-500 KT); the 1967 directive runs
  DUAL timelines with the conventional track as the *slower
  option* and names Karup — BALTAP's own headquarters — as a DGZ;
  the 1983-era 5. Armee plan opened with up to 32 strikes
  (php-maritime-front.md; oob-verification.md).
- **Release sits two echelons above our front, both sides.** Soviet
  release only by the Supreme Commander of the Unified Armed
  Forces (1964 dossier); the Polish front fights under a nuclear
  plan it will never own — Warsaw wasn't even told about the
  Soviet Zealand package (zealand-landing.md). Blue: MC 14/3
  flexible response means SACEUR request → political consultation
  measured in days, with a fodnotepolitik-era Danish government in
  the loop for anything on or over Danish soil.
- **The IC's nuclear-readiness clock (2 days) is shorter than its
  conventional-generation clock (~2 weeks)**
  (nie-threat-estimates.md) — the fast lane exists and everyone
  knows it.

So "why doesn't anyone use it?" is not a background question on
this axis; it has documented owners, clocks, and paperwork. That
is a problem and an opportunity.

## 2. The trope map — how the genre holds the door shut

*(From general knowledge; Hackett, Clancy, Coyle are purchase-list
items not yet held — verify the map against copies when they
arrive. Mechanisms, with exemplars:)*

- **A. Rational-aims filter** (*Red Storm Rising*): the war aim
  (intact resources) is incompatible with nuclear use, so red
  self-deters from the top; blue reciprocates because it never
  loses badly enough to face its own first-use doctrine. Clean,
  but it quietly requires blue's conventional defense to succeed —
  the conceit is secretly a plot constraint.
- **B. Mutual-hostage statement, made once**: a seminar/briefing
  scene establishes deterrence stalemate, then the book ignores
  it. Cheap, honest, and inert — the door is shut but nobody in
  the story is holding it.
- **C. Offstage inheritance** (*Team Yankee*): the strategic
  question is decided above the book's altitude; company-level
  aperture never owns it. **Unavailable to us**: our protagonist
  sits in the headquarters where release requests would be
  drafted. We cannot keep the question offstage; we must own it.
- **D. Speed / termination before decision** (Peters, *Red Army*):
  the war ends (politically) before the escalation machinery
  completes a cycle. Structurally elegant; requires a short war —
  which we have — but as sole mechanism it reads as luck.
- **E. Demonstrative exchange as terminator** (Hackett:
  Birmingham/Minsk): one exchange ends the book. Violates DK's
  constraint (exercised, not withheld); off the table.
- **F. Chemical as the permitted transgression** (Hackett, Coyle:
  WP chemical use while nuclear holds): genre precedent AGAINST
  our constraint — the neighbors let chemical through. If we
  withhold chemical too, we diverge from genre precedent and need
  a reason, not a shrug (see §5, weakest-leg note).

## 3. Candidate picks (alternatives forced, per process rules)

1. **RSR-style aims filter, stated once** (A+B): red's limited aim
   (the Straits seized intact, a fait accompli before NATO
   coheres) makes nuclear use self-defeating; one scene says so.
   Cost: secretly constrains the plot (blue must never quite
   lose); the lampshade recurs every time blue nearly does.
2. **Pure speed** (D): the 2-week/2-day clock asymmetry plus a
   short war — release cycles simply don't complete. Cost: reads
   as authorial luck; and our own instrument keeps generating
   near-collapse branches where someone WOULD reach for the fast
   lane.
3. **Non-use as the load-bearing operational fact** (novel;
   recommended — see §4): fuse A, B, D and F's refusal into the
   book's own throughput frame, with both sides' withholding
   given owners, clocks, and running operational meaning.

## 4. THE PICK (locked 2026-07-22, DK directive in-session;
## design as below, DK may amend)

**Rule:** No nuclear, chemical, or biological employment by either
side within this book. Biological is flatly off-table (period
doctrine itself treats it as strategic/uncontrollable; BWC 1972) —
zero decision-tree presence. Nuclear and chemical remain live,
visible, PRICED branches in both sides' decision trees — and are
not exercised.

**Mechanism (the novel part): non-use is operationally
load-bearing, on the book's own terms — tempo.**

- **Red is running its slow plan and knows it.** The 1967
  directive's dual timelines make red's opening choice legible:
  a conventional H-hour is red *choosing the conventional
  timeline* — limited-aims logic (the Straits and the Kiel Canal
  locks wanted INTACT, per its own 1961 directive; a fait
  accompli that gives NATO's nuclear consultation nothing
  unambiguous to convene over). From day one, red's staff
  measures its shortfall against the nuclear norm line it didn't
  take. Red's nuclear option appears in the book as a
  *measuring stick* — "we are four days behind the other
  timeline" — never as fires. The overconfident plant manager
  keeps declining the forbidden shortcut because taking it burns
  the plant down; that IS the Goal frame absorbing the bomb.
- **Blue's conventional performance is what keeps its own question
  unasked.** Under flexible response, blue's nuclear question
  arrives precisely when the corps fails conventionally. The G-3's
  throughput problem is therefore the non-escalation mechanism
  itself: the corps' unstated mission is to keep the release
  request a draft. (The draft can exist — a document the staff
  revises and never sends — that is the one running visible token
  of the branch, and it is paperwork, exactly this book's
  register.)
- **Both release clocks are slower than the decisive phase, and
  starting them early is itself escalation.** Soviet release two
  echelons up; NATO consultation in days, with a Danish footnote
  government holding a practical veto over anything Danish — the
  fodnotepolitik irony: the policy that weakens deterrence
  guarantees the local fight stays conventional. The 90-hour
  clock has a nuclear sibling nobody can afford to start.
- **Chemical, the weakest leg, held by red's own economics:**
  MOPP and decontamination are tempo killers, and this front's
  objectives (ports, locks, airfields red needs operating) are
  exactly what persistent agents poison. Red withholds chemical
  for quota-system reasons (consumption-factors.md §5 logic:
  chemical expenditure is an *assigned* resource for the
  breakthrough that needs it — and the plan says this one
  doesn't). Honest flag: period NATO belief (FM 100-2-1) treats
  Soviet chemical as integrated, blue in-theater retaliation is
  near-nil, and the genre neighbors let chemical through — so
  this leg is authorial rule dressed in a defensible red
  argument, TO-VERIFY against the 1967 directive's conventional
  track (does it carry chemical? — open item) and permitted ONE
  in-book lampshade, once, in the same scene as the rest.
- **Say it once, then silence.** The whole logic above is earned
  in ONE scene (the estimate/seminar where G-2 and G-3 walk both
  sides' trees — the four staged questions naturally carry it:
  what is the goal / what does the enemy think it is / ...), and
  thereafter the book does not lampshade. Recurring nuclear
  anxiety is register-breaking; the door was shown locked, with
  the keys visible on both belts.

## 5. The escape clause, made concrete

DK's "barring a very good argument" is operationalized so it can't
creep: the conceit yields only if the instrument shows the
campaign's canonical corridor EXITS the band where both sides'
non-use logic holds — i.e., a sweep-robust branch where red's
conventional timeline fails so badly so early that limited-aims
logic collapses, or blue's line breaks past the political hold
with reinforcement clocks unstarted. Such branches are marked
**"outside the conceit envelope"** in the findings log and become
scenario-validity findings (re-scope the scenario), not in-book
escalations. If a re-scoped scenario STILL exits the envelope,
that is an Andon Cord conversation with DK, not a drafting
decision.

## 6. Consequences

- **Instrument:** NBC is never simulated as fires. Two cheap
  additions queued for the re-baseline campaign, both
  measurement-only: (a) red norm-shortfall vs the nuclear
  timeline as a reported metric (the 1967 dual-timeline phase
  lines give the yardstick); (b) a conceit-envelope validity
  check per run (flags, not mechanics).
- **War origin constraint:** whatever origin is chosen must be
  compatible with red rationally choosing the conventional track
  at H-hour (limited aims, fait accompli, seize-intact
  objectives). This now constrains the origin design alongside
  setting-time criterion 7.
- **Scene budget:** one earned NBC scene (Phase-1 outline slots
  it); one chemical lampshade allowed inside it; zero elsewhere.
- **Research adds (fetch/purchase queue):** period nuclear
  release-procedure literature — Bracken, *The Command and
  Control of Nuclear Forces* (1983, period-exact); NPG/MC 14/3
  open literature; Danish nuclear-policy specifics (peacetime
  no-nukes vs wartime ambiguity — DIIS vols carry some, partially
  mined); whether the 1967 conventional track includes chemical
  (facsimile re-read, targeted).
- **Reader-contract check:** the conceit is period-honest — real
  staffs planned conventional campaigns inside a nuclear frame
  daily without resolving it; treating non-use as procedure and
  tempo rather than luck is more authentic, not less.
