# Premise and design sketch

*Captured at stub time (2026-07-20). Everything here is provisional until
Phase 1 (planning) actually runs.*

## The premise

Goldratt's *The Goal*, but the plant is a NATO corps and the throughput is
tempo. A staff officer (the Alex Rogo figure) in a failing defense against a
Warsaw Pact offensive, circa 1986, discovers operational art the way Rogo
discovers the Theory of Constraints — through misleading metrics, a Socratic
mentor (the Jonah figure), and painful application under pressure.

The didactic payload is AirLand Battle doctrine and its intellectual
surroundings: attrition metrics that mislead; echelonment as the constraint;
deep battle / follow-on forces attack; tempo as throughput; recon-strike;
sortie generation; the OODA loop. Arc shape: failing defense → doctrinal
insight → applied counterstroke.

Setting is *Red Storm Rising*-like as vibe only — the shared WWIII-'80s genre.
Original characters, original events, no borrowed furniture.

## Why this premise for the 50–60k increment

- The didactic form gives every chapter a load-bearing payload; length is
  carried honestly. (White Buffalo's lesson: its 60k target collapsed to 31k
  because the story was a 31k story. This one is naturally 50–60k.)
- Plays to the method's certified strengths: procedural concreteness, material
  culture, lean dialogue, restraint.
- The named craft increment over White Buffalo's dual-voice: an ensemble
  (staff-section shape) and sustained plot logistics.

## Method notes ported from white-buffalo

- Build the ledgers in Phase 1, not as a retrofit: timeline, geography,
  continuity, and — new for this genre — an order-of-battle ledger.
- The undershoot problem compounds at this scale: ~25–30 units each quietly
  15% thin is a missing 8k words. Per-unit word floors; thinness is diagnosed
  by the human, named specifically.
- Instrument suite transfers wholesale; re-cast the auditor personas:
  - doctrine auditor (replaces period auditor)
  - hardware/material auditor
  - veteran reader (era-appropriate service experience)
  - Clancy-pastiche detector
  - "PowerPoint-in-prose" reader (flags didactic seams)
- Process rules from White Buffalo's model-author profile are presumed to
  apply to any drafting model: self-review is not clearance; evaluations are
  briefs, not verdicts; alternatives must be forced; length reports are
  unreliable. A fresh critique profile of the actual drafting model must be
  built early — the Opus 4.5 profile does not transfer verbatim.

## Open questions for Phase 1

- Echelon of the protagonist: corps staff vs division staff vs brigade — what
  level makes the doctrinal payloads *experienced* rather than briefed?
- Who is Jonah? (A retired officer? A wargamer? An enemy-doctrine analyst?)
- Front: CENTAG/NORTHAG Germany is the genre default; is there a fresher
  theater that keeps the doctrine legible?
- How overt is the didacticism — The Goal's unapologetic seminar scenes, or
  something more submerged?
- Title. `the-mission-1986` is the working repo name; "The Mission" riffs on
  The Goal but is not settled.
