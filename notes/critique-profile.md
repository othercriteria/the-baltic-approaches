and CLAUDE.md process rule 5 (fresh critique profile before
drafting). Sources: this session's own close read

## 1. Where to trust the model

- **The epistemic war.** Staff cognition, logistics-as-constraint,
  the political gating of force, culmination, refusal-of-
  exploitation — "genuinely expert, and rarely rendered" (doctrine
  auditor). The morning-market education, the valve, the two-clock
  structure: this is the model's home ground.
- **Administrative and procedural texture.** Command chain
  (LANDJUT→BALTAP→AFNORTH), ranks and address in four armies,
  signals formats, map-room practice, mobilization-reporting
  pathology, the political backdrop dated to the month. Verified
  clean by both auditors.
- **Anti-pastiche instincts.** The pastiche detector's verdict:
  the draft "refuses the entire payoff structure of the genre" —
  no battle spectacle, the climax is waiting on purpose, the
  nuclear brink-scene is declined by the enemy on professional
  grounds, and the book audits its own instrument ("we were not
  right; we were solvent"). "Exactly the note pastiche cannot
  hit." This originality is the asset every revision pass must
  protect.
- **Embodied abstraction, when it stops there.** The bridge's
  empty seconds, the clipboard exchange rate, the confession wall
  as object, the folder audited aloud — the didactic reader's
  honor roll. The model's best move is giving a concept hands.

## 2. Signature failure modes (each with countermeasure)

**2.1 The payload-and-stop reflex (the 30k attractor's mechanism).**
Drives every unit to its quotable payload and stops; "done" =
argument landed. Same reflex at every scale: chapter length,
the 20-for-20 closing epigram, the boxed notebook lines.
*Countermeasure:* per-unit conversion targets (allocation sheet);
epigram rationed to ~1 chapter in 3; some chapters end unresolved.

**2.2 Double delivery — the model distrusts the reader.**
The didactic reader's core finding: it dramatizes a lesson
competently, then restates it — chapter-terminal narrator gloss
(ch. 9's coda after Loheide already taught it), a licensed mouth
(Rahn the oracle, Aakjær the chorus), prolepsis stamps ("did not
know he was logging the first move of the argument that would
decide the campaign"), self-grading vocabulary ("the education,"
"the lesson," "the trap"). The deepest habit: **making
significance explicit** — foreshadowing it, naming its own
symmetries, captioning its own themes.
*Countermeasure (mechanical, subtractive):* delete the last
paragraph of roughly half the chapters; retire
education/lesson/trap as narrator vocabulary; cut all prolepsis;
ration Rahn to fewer contacts that confirm in retrospect rather
than preview; the reader-ahead audit table checks each station.

**2.3 The reader-ahead mechanism breaks in the middle third.**
Chs. 1–9 and 15–20 hold (reader ahead of or with the staff);
chs. 10–14 break — Rahn and the off-page-re-auditing Aakjær solve
the next concept offscreen and present it finished, dragging the
reader BEHIND. The book's two supreme acts of method (Rylski's
reconciliation, Aakjær's re-founding) are both delivered as
orations after unseen work.
*Countermeasure:* stage at least one audit AS PROCESS; let the
reframe surface from an on-stage anomaly a half-beat before the
character names it.

**2.4 One beautiful voice, everyone's.** Confirmed by every
instrument independently: Hvidt, Rahn, Aakjær, Zawadzki, Marei,
Loheide all deliver the same polished self-diagnosing epigram;
"the prose has one beautiful voice and it belongs to the author."
Distinctness probe: Rahn identifiable (Socratic-refusal
structure), Aakjær mostly (exits, dryness), Hvidt only by content
signature, Vossen not at all in dialogue. Tonal range exists;
REGISTER range does not.
*Countermeasure:* E1's monologue→argument conversion; per-
character speech rules in the outline (who is permitted an
aphorism, who never completes one, who is boring on purpose);
Marei and junior officers must NOT be wits.

**2.5 Epistemic competence porn.** Tactical competence porn
inverted (boards lie, models wrong) but smuggled back one level
up: every Socratic prompt lands, every insight arrives on
schedule, nobody is stupid, venal, careerist, or confused past
one scene; the protagonist is never personally wrong after ch. 3.
*Countermeasure:* the F0–F7 ramp (allocation §1) + charging at
least one failure to Vossen's own signed judgment over an on-page
objection (close-read §3).

**2.6 The physical-hardware competence boundary.** The hardware
auditor's profile: the model renders organization and procedure
confidently and correctly, and "pivots to a functional noun
exactly where a hardware name would be required" — systematic
ignorance-management. When it does commit to a physical
infrastructure fact unverified, that is where it breaks: the
Great Belt BRIDGE that didn't exist until 1997-98 (ferry-only in
1983 — and the ferry is the better story: the real valve for
moving island forces west), Reichsbahn for a West German G-4
(Bundesbahn), "E45" in 1983 (period usage E3 — the long-flagged
anachronism confirmed), "one bridge" over the Little Belt (two by
1970), photostat/ozone-teleprinter texture. Unnamed everywhere:
armor, artillery, bridging kit, the amphibious hulls, radios —
all cheap wins sitting in the shelf.
*Countermeasure:* the hardware pass at revision runs WITH the
shelf and the OOB ledger; draft one names kit only from ledger
entries, never from the model's memory.

**2.7 Map-vocabulary without map-logic (the big one).** The
doctrine auditor's blocker: the specimen opens the war at the
DANISH border (Ellund, Wassersleben, axes Flensburg→Schleswig
pointing south) — the wrong end of the peninsula — while its own
deep targets sit correctly southeast of Lübeck. The model "knows
the map's vocabulary but not the map's logic, and will
confidently route an army in from the wrong border." Related
real catches: the Jutland Division doesn't need the Little Belt
to reach Schleswig (it mobilizes IN Jutland; only Funen/Zealand
elements cross water); the Kiel Canal never functions as a
defense line in the specimen's battle geometry.
*Countermeasure:* the geography/map ledger (CLAUDE.md Phase-1
list) becomes a drafting-time input, not a revision-time check —
draft one's outline carries an axis-and-front-trace map per Part
before any scene is written; every movement sentence checked
against it.

**2.8 Tidy, personalized, symmetric mechanisms.** The model
imports a rational-actor operations-research sensibility into
every headquarters (red reads like "a Western analyst in Polish
uniform"), reaches for clean binaries (slow=conventional /
fast=nuclear), and rhymes everything (every plant pays, every
symmetry resolves — close-read §6).
*Countermeasure:* red's norm-bound schematic culture from the
shelf (soviet-operational-art, php-maritime-front) written into
R-beat craft notes; two or three plants left deliberately unpaid;
one symmetry made to misfire.

**2.9 Verbal tic inventory (pastiche detector's counts).**
Em-dash aside ~470 across 20 chs (~23/ch); "Not X. Y." dozens;
tricolon dozens; "the way a man..." ~30; "X, which was Y" ~45
(front-loaded, 8 in ch. 1); disguise-metaphor ("wearing/dressed
as"); coins imagery; ledger vocabulary saturation (ledger 19,
bank/banked 21, priced 16, audit 16); one simile reused verbatim
("like a man setting down a glass," chs. 1 and 7). Load-bearing
(ration, don't remove): em-dash respiration, ledger conceit,
Not-X-Y. Removable: weather-closers, coins, half the "the way a
man" similes, all verbatim reuse.
*Countermeasure:* per-chapter tic budget in the outline craft
notes; a grep-able tic checklist at revision.

## 3. Instrument calibration (what this pass taught about auditing)

Fresh-context instruments without shelf access re-open questions
the research has settled — useful as coverage proof, noise if
taken as verdicts. This pass produced three such: (a) "COMLANDJUT
was fixed-German" — false; the alternation is documented
researched furniture and the Danish incoming commander is
declared deviation #1; (b) "chemical was separable from nuclear,
a conventional WP offensive would use gas" — the 1967 facsimile
shows red's own plan bundling chemical under ОМП, primary-sourced
(nbc-conceit); (c) "the methodical enemy contradicts OMG-era
doctrine" — the researched SH-axis architecture and the NIEs'
documented two-phase sequence are the project's answer, and
Zawadzki's declination scene is its on-page defense.
**Rule for revision-time audits: pair every fresh-eyes instrument
with a shelf-informed verifier before any finding becomes a
change.** The auditors' genuinely new catches this round: the
wrong-border opening (2.7), the Great Belt bridge (2.6), the
absent deputy commander AND the absent Chief of Staff (Witzel is
in the specimen's own cast list and appears in zero chapters),
SACEUR elided from the release chain, NATO alert-state vocabulary
unused, W-day vs M-day/D-day convention unpinned, corps G-3 rank
(Oberstleutnant may be a grade light for the principal G-3 —
protagonist-echelon.md says "Oberstleutnant-equivalent"; verify
or make him the deputy who inherits the chair).

## 4. Standing craft rules for draft one (the profile's output)

1. Geometry from the map ledger; kit from the OOB ledger; nothing
   physical from memory.
2. A stake in the room in every spine chapter; a felt-lateness
   beat where comprehension is the drama; the unsaid rendered
   free-indirect (matrix judging §2.3).
3. Epigram ration 1-in-3; no prolepsis; no education/lesson
   vocabulary; half the chapters end without a curtain-line.
4. Monologue→argument everywhere the allocation sheet says E1;
   one audit staged as process (2.3).
5. Per-character speech rules; the aphorism is a rationed
   substance and Rahn holds most of the ration.
6. Failures charged to named signatures including the
   protagonist's; the reader stays ahead through Part III.
7. Plants: two or three left unpaid, chosen in outline.
8. Red drafted norm-bound from the shelf, not rational-actor.
