In November 1983 the war comes to the Baltic Approaches — the
narrow ground where the Jutland peninsula meets the North German
Plain, and the water on either side of it. *The Baltic Approaches*
follows the operations staff of a binational NATO corps at
Rendsburg through that war, November into December. It is a staff
novel: the ground is an operations room, the instruments are
estimates, and the subject is what a headquarters can honestly
claim to know. The corps posts its estimates on a wall, prices its
own ignorance daily, and settles the account when the bill
arrives. Played straight, without hindsight: the staff knows only
what it has learned, and pays for the rest.

> We were not right. We were solvent.
>
> — from the corps' final assessment
