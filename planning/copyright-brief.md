# Copyright registration — the application, mapped

*Session 94f69afb, 2026-08-01, DK-directed ("let's map out more
completely the copyright application process"). Doctrine and
sources: reference/copyright-ai.md + shelf batch 11. Standing
ruling absorbed: **no salting the finished text with human touches
to strengthen the claim** (DK, this date). Frame: the filing is an
experiment conducted honestly — the registration if granted, and
the examiner correspondence either way, are record artifacts of
the same kind this project has kept throughout.*

## 0. The decision, stated

File a Standard Application for the literary work, claiming
exactly the human layer and excluding the AI-generated text,
with the ledger available as the supporting record. Outcome
genuinely uncertain (see §5); cost bounded (~$65 + two deposit
copies + postage); the deadline that matters is the §412 window
(§4). A refusal does not extinguish any rights that exist and
does not preclude suit (§411(a) allows filing suit after refusal
with notice to the Register); it produces a reasoned letter that
joins the record.

## 1. Route and form

- **Standard Application** (electronic, eCO at copyright.gov;
  fee $65 — verify at filing). The **Single Application** ($45)
  is ineligible: it requires all content to be authored by the
  one individual author-claimant, which the exclusions here
  preclude.
- Type of work: **Literary Work (TX class)**.
- Title: *The Baltic Approaches: A Novel of November 1983*.
  ISBNs on hand: 979-8-9973189-0-1 (paperback),
  979-8-9973189-1-8 (eBook). One registration covers the work;
  editions with identical text do not need separate filings.

## 2. The fields that matter (drafted)

- **Author:** Daniel Klein (US citizen). AI is not listed as an
  author (88 FR 16190 practice). Work made for hire: NO.
- **Author Created ("describe this author's contribution"):**
  "Selection, coordination, and arrangement of text generated by
  artificial intelligence; editorial direction and revision
  rulings throughout." Keep it accurate and spare — this field
  is the claim.
- **Limitation of Claim — Material Excluded:** "Text generated
  by artificial intelligence; map plates generated by artificial
  intelligence; cover art derived from a public-domain painting
  (CC0, Metropolitan Museum of Art)."
- **Limitation of Claim — New Material Included:** "Selection,
  coordination, and arrangement; editorial direction."
- **Note to Copyright Office field (use it):** two or three
  sentences, e.g.: "The narrative text was drafted by Anthropic
  Claude models under the applicant's direction; the making is
  documented at session grain in a public repository
  (github.com/othercriteria/the-baltic-approaches, incl. a
  per-session attribution ledger and raw transcripts). The claim
  is limited to the applicant's selection, coordination,
  arrangement, and editorial direction. The applicant is
  available for any inquiry." Honesty up front converts the
  likely examiner correspondence into a shorter conversation and
  forecloses any later §411(b) question.
- **Publication:** published, YES. **Date of first publication:**
  the paperback's first go-live date — DK to confirm from the
  KDP dashboard (the record brackets it 2026-07-29..31; the
  original submission went live before the errata resubmission).
  Nation: United States.
- **Claimant:** Daniel Klein (same as author; no transfer).

## 3. Deposit

- Published-in-print work → **best edition = the printed
  paperback; two complete physical copies** mailed to the Office
  with the eCO shipping slip. (Electronic-only deposit is for
  works published only electronically; the paperback forecloses
  that convenience.) Practical: the author copies already on
  order can seed this — order two more.
- The §407 mandatory LoC deposit (two best-edition copies within
  three months of publication, on demand) is **satisfied by the
  registration deposit** — one more reason the filing is nearly
  free in real terms.

## 4. Timing

- **Effective date of registration = the date the Office
  receives** application + fee + deposit in acceptable form.
- **§412 window:** statutory damages and attorney's fees for
  post-publication infringements are preserved only if the
  effective date lands **within three months of first
  publication** — i.e., roughly **by end of October 2026**
  (exact day pins to the publication date DK confirms). After
  the window, registration still works; only the remedies
  premium lapses.
- Processing: months (typical 2–8+); AI-involved claims
  frequently draw correspondence. The effective date, not the
  certificate date, is what the window cares about — file, then
  wait without anxiety.

## 5. Expected examination and the refusal path

- Likely: an examiner letter asking to confirm the extent of AI
  generation and the human contribution. The answer is the Note
  field's content, expanded; the ledger is citable but need not
  be attached wholesale.
- The precedent split (copyright-ai.md §1.4) suggests a properly
  limited claim CAN issue (*Zarya*'s arrangement; the *American
  Cheese* grant on selection/arrangement alone) — and that the
  honest complication here (arrangement itself substantially
  AI-proposed, human-ratified) is exactly the unexamined
  question. Genuine possible outcomes: grant as filed; grant
  narrowed; refusal.
- If refused: **First Request for Reconsideration** ($350,
  within 3 months of refusal) → **Second Request / Review Board**
  ($700) → APA action in federal district court (the Thaler
  path). Each step optional and separable; each produces a
  reasoned public document. Recommendation: take at least the
  first reconsideration if refused on a ground the ledger
  answers; stop before litigation unless DK develops independent
  appetite.

## 6. What a grant would and would not do

- Would: register the compilation-layer claim (the book as an
  arranged whole), unlock federal suit + the §412 remedies for
  that layer, and put a US government determination about this
  project's authorship structure into the record.
- Would not: protect any sentence of the prose as such. Copying
  passages remains legally unimpeded in the US; wholesale
  republication of the arranged whole is the only conduct the
  registration plausibly reaches. The LICENSE's amended
  copyright-status note states this to readers directly.
- Not available regardless: LCCN/PCN (pre-publication program;
  moot), Authors Guild certification (certifies the opposite
  fact).
