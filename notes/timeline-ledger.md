## The clock (ratified, outline §2)

- **M-day** = Sun **6 Nov 1983**: BALTAP warning order 0640;
  alert measure 060955Z. The staff's **ninety-hour clock runs
  from 060955Z** (H+90 = Thu 10 Nov ~0400 — hence Lammers'
  "Where is the division on Thursday morning?" at the midnight
  conference, ch. 3).
- **Day 1** = Tue **8 Nov**, first contact 080351Z (= H+42).
  War-days count from first contact; red's operation days count
  from the same date (red Day 1 = 8 Nov).
- Dates are primary in prose; war-day ordinals derived. Weekday
  arithmetic: 6/13/20/27 Nov = Sundays; 27 Nov = **First Advent**
  (almanac; red's great effort deliberately falls on it).
