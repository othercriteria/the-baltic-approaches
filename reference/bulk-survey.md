# /bulk/dlk/military survey (2026-07-20)

Three-agent sweep of DK's local archive (22,008 files, 15 GB) for
low-hanging fruit. Character of the collection: survivalebooks.com
repackages (~2009) of genuine US Army correspondence-course and FM
scans — underlying documents are US-government public domain, but
the scan packaging is commercial and watermarked. **Use in place
(paths below); do not copy into this repo or redistribute the
scans.** Where we need a clean public copy, the fetch queue sources
the same document from CARL/archive.org instead.

## Top pulls (by project need)

**Doctrine spine — new to us:**
- FM 100-5 **1976** (Active Defense itself — was on our fetch queue,
  sits in `6 Vietnam War Library/1976 US Army Operations FM 100 5/`
  in 3 parts). The legacy doctrine the periphery still runs; the
  protagonist's seniors' formative text.
- FM 100-2-**2** (1984, Soviet specialized warfare/rear area) and
  FM 100-2-**3** (1991, Soviet troops/organization/equipment) —
  complete the FM 100-2 trilogy with the -2-1 we fetched. -2-3 is
  the Red OOB bench reference the ledger needs.
- FM 100-5 1982 also present (`9 Leadership/1982 US Army OPERATIONS
  189p.pdf`) — we already hold a clean copy; noted for provenance.
- Period-exact 1986 division and brigade/battalion IEW manuals
  (FM 34-10/34-80 family); Rear Battle FM 90-14 (1985);
  Survivability FM 5-103 (1985); corps/division fire support (1989);
  Corps Operations FM 100-15 (1996, descendant construct).

**The Goal's own texture — the rail cluster** (`12 Railroad
Operations/`, ~15 files, 1969-76, read whole): *Rail Ops Planning*
(1970) is literally a course in computing the bottleneck capacity of
a theater rail network (tonnage capacity determination, drawbar
pull, line capacity); *Theater Railroad* (1976) speaks COSCOM/COMMZ
at exactly a corps sustainment officer's altitude; *Dispatching* is
real-time sequencing of a constrained line; *Yard* is the
marshalling bottleneck made physical. Goldratt in uniform, and
period. The strongest single find of the survey.

**Radio discipline** (`8 Survival Communications/`, ~60 files):
Tactical Single-Channel Radio Techniques (1987, 323p) for net
procedure and prowords; Combat Net Radio Operations (1990);
"Communications in a Come as You Are War" (1990); brevity codes,
visual signals, jamming. The sound of the book's message traffic.
Gap: exact-period FM 24-18 → fetch queue.

**Design/aesthetic bench (for in-novel props and the book's
document idiom):**
- Topographic symbols plates, 1961 + 1964 — the maps' visual grammar.
- FM 101-5-1 Operational Terms & Graphics (1997; symbology stable
  from the 80s) — overlay/unit-symbol reference.
- FM 101-5 Staff Organization and Operations (1997 ed.) — OPORD/
  annex/estimate formats the protagonist produces.
- REPORTS AND FORMS (1992) — reproducible fill-in-the-boxes layouts.
- Standard military vehicle characteristic data sheets (1963, 306p)
  — the data-sheet form aesthetic AND raw movement/loading numbers.
- The **Aggressor** series (1959-60: Handbook on Aggressor Military
  Forces; Aggressor: The Maneuver Enemy) — the Army's invented
  exercise enemy with fictional ORBAT/symbology/uniforms. Direct
  inspiration for exercise scenes and for how a training enemy
  differs from the real one (thematically loaded for us).
- Overlay-preparation courses (1994, 2001) — how overlays are
  actually constructed and registered.
- Caveat: period scans carry a ghosted survivalebooks watermark +
  MHI seal; any prop reproduction needs cleanup or a clean re-source.

**Logistics texture beyond rail:** pipeline scheduling/batching
(1998, Goldratt-shaped), FSSP/POL operations family, Class V/ammo
supply point series (1988-92), convoy ops (1991, built on FM 55-30
1980 — the period parent goes on the fetch queue), theater supply
concepts (1993), 1966 Transportation Planning & Movement Control
(the nearest stand-in for the missing FM 101-10 planning factors).
