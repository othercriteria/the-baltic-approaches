## 0. The methods experiment: skim vs. full read

Same lens (literary staff-vantage), same candidate pool, one agent
reading chs. 9 and 16 plus premise docs (47k tokens), one reading
the entire book first (150k tokens). Verdict: **the full read is
better, not merely different — it moved the map's center of
gravity** — but the skim found things the full read missed, so the
union is the map.

What only the full read saw:
- **The book is not single-POV.** Chapters 4/11/17/21 are a second,
  complete ~8k-word staff novel (Rylski/Zawadzki), ch. 7 closes
  inside Frau Lohse's car, and a documentary-retrospective register
  ("the report survives") runs through the text. This moved the
  whole map from the single-consciousness management-novel shelf to
  the **chronicle/document-fiction axis** (Deighton, Kluge,
  Plievier, Solzhenitsyn) and demoted the comedy/chamber-piece
  comps the premise implies (Waugh, Heller, Fobbit → contrast).
- **The ethic as dramatized is The Goal's refutation, not its
  transposition.** Jonah's method works; Rahn refuses to bless, the
  wall is wrong at the crest, three of seven numbered failures
  carry Holt's initials, and the after-action verdict is "We were
  not right. We were solvent." The finished text belongs with the
  novels of institutional honesty as a priced moral practice
  (Cozzens, Grossman, Solzhenitsyn), not the optimization novels.
- Ch. 22's archival-annex fight unlocked the Wouk "World Empire
  Lost"/von Roon comp (the contest over the operational record as
  plot) — unreachable from any summary.
What only the skim found: Haines' *Command Decision*, Ford's
*Parade's End*, Balchin's *The Small Back Room* — breadth the
full-read agent's context budget apparently crowded out.

Lesson for future instrument design: for register/vantage judgments
the full read is authoritative where the two disagree; run skim
agents for sweep breadth and one full-read agent as the regrader.

## 2. Positioning, synthesized (four lens claims, mutually consistent)

1. **Genre (future-war):** goal-likes.md's empty-slot claim
   SURVIVES the 2024–26 sweep, refined to three axes: TBA sits at
   the unclaimed intersection of **Peters' altitude × Goldratt's
   engine × Kluge's distance**. Nearest occupants: Red Army /
   White Sun War (altitude, no engine); the Alekseyev thread in
   Red Storm Rising is the nearest *character* (an operational
   deputy in an inherited chair). The distance axis is not merely
   unoccupied but unattempted — the genre's commercial gradient
   runs downhill toward contact (Gragg, the clean case: EUCOM
   staff access, wrote a rifle novel). The Baltic Approaches at
   corps-staff altitude has no fictional occupant; Able Archer
   month has one (Morra, a spy novel).
2. **Didactic lineage:** method-altitude at operational echelon is
   still empty after 2025 — including *Delivering DUFFER* (Spring
   2025, Gene Kim co-author: the Goldratt and Swinton lineages
   welded a year before TBA, at bureaucratic altitude, still
   reaching for the dream engine). TBA's formal invention: the
   **posted estimate reconciled against outcome** is the Prussian
   applicatory method (Griepenkerl 1890, Verdy du Vernois) — 135
   years of general-staff pedagogy that had the mechanism and
   never put a person inside it. Only *Flatland* shares the bet
   that a single restricted viewpoint is itself the pedagogy.
3. **Literary tradition:** the staff-novel line (Ford, Cozzens,
   Balchin, Haines, Solzhenitsyn, Grossman) relocates the moral
   act into the record — and near-universally finds honest
   measurement professionally fatal (tragedy). TBA's new position:
   candor discovered to be **instrumentally superior and rewarded,
   at exactly one echelon, without rescue** — record-keeping as
   jurisdiction, "more Cozzens than anyone, with a doctrinal
   engine Cozzens never wanted."
4. **Process space:** TBA occupies an **empty quadrant** — full
   public process record + priced + self-published + making kept
   off the sales surface. The session-grain ledger with per-entity
   signed statements has no located precedent as of 2026-08; the
   models' standing to review/amend the making's account before
   publication is without parallel. Caveat on the record: negative
   search results are weak in unindexed self-pub space (Checkpoint
   itself has zero press). The Authors Guild "Human Authored"
   registry is the competing ledger — certifying the opposite
   fact; no counterpart institution exists on the disclosed side.
   The Shy Girl withdrawal (Orbit, 2026-03) is the market case
   that a kept provenance record is now also a commercial defense.
