# Provenance

Idea selected 2026-07-20, in a Claude Code session (Claude Fable 5; session
`656ec2ba-b295-4e5d-8712-5e270300dcde`) with DK, immediately after the release
of White Buffalo v1.0 (github.com/othercriteria/white-buffalo).

The session reviewed the finished book and its method, then evaluated four
candidate premises DK had been carrying for the next increment (a work at
50–60k words, up from White Buffalo's ~31k):

1. Alt-history: Chinese settlement in the Antebellum US concentrated in New
   Orleans, Taiping-flavored — judged the strongest *book* but oversized at
   full scope and carrying a fresh cultural-sensitivity research load;
   banked for later as "a study within the premise" (e.g., one narrator, one
   bounded event such as the 1853 yellow fever summer).
2. **The Goal for military doctrine (this repo)** — chosen. Rationale: the
   didactic form gives every chapter a doctrinal payload, which carries 50–60k
   honestly (White Buffalo's lesson: don't inflate a novella); it plays
   directly to the method's certified strengths (procedural concreteness,
   material culture, lean dialogue, restraint); the research burden is heavy
   but well-documented and suits the instrument/audit method; low copyright
   friction; no cultural-sensitivity minefield.
3. Star Trek Dominion War ASW fanfic (escort-craft POV, convoy doctrine) —
   recognized as the same doctrinal itch as #2 in a different uniform, and
   incompatible with the released-artifact pipeline (fanfic can't be the
   milestone work product). Possible noncommercial side-serial someday.
4. "Rat-ical Honesty," a Pixar-style screenplay — wrong scale for the goal
   (a feature screenplay is ~20–25k words); parked as a short-form pipeline
   experiment (screenplay format + storyboards via the image pipeline).

Two further ideas surfaced in the same session and were stubbed alongside this
repo: a Great Lakes wreck novel (`november-gale`) and a telegraph/Carrington
event novel (`the-wires-1859`).
