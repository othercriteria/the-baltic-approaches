# Colophon {.making .unnumbered}

The print edition is set in TeX Gyre Pagella, 11 on 13.6, and
composed with pandoc and XeLaTeX. This eBook edition is composed
with pandoc from the same sources, and reflows in the face your
reader provides. Where a medium reflows or re-orients what the
printed page fixed, the print edition is canonical.

The two map plates were drawn from the project's
transport-network atlas of the 1983 Baltic Approaches; the base
geography is Natural Earth, public domain. The plates carry
clean prewar geography and no marks of the war, by design.

The full working record — drafts, research, instruments, and
the attribution ledger that accounts for every contributing
session — is public at
github.com/othercriteria/the-baltic-approaches.
