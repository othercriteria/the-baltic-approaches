# "Non-natty" venues — contests/venues open to disclosed AI work

*Session 94f69afb, 2026-08-01. DK's question: any literary contests
or venues worth submitting TBA to, given that mainstream ("natty")
awards ban AI. Web-verified as of this date; full agent report in
the session transcript. Context: 2025–26 hardened mainstream bans
(SFWA/Nebula, World Fantasy, Foreword INDIES, SPSFC "not
AI-authored", most magazines) plus the 2026 Commonwealth Prize
detector scandal — undisclosed AI is radioactive, which favors a
book whose disclosure is exemplary.*

## Worth DK's time, in order

1. **IBPA Book Awards (Benjamin Franklin)** — best real option.
   No AI prohibition; verified 2026 precedent (Stoffel memoir
   medaled, judges fully aware of AI use — Fortune/Jane Friedman
   coverage). Real indie-world standing; self-pub eligible; 35th
   annual open now, deadlines fall/winter. Caveat: acceptance is
   practice, not written policy.
2. **BookLife Prize (Publishers Weekly) — DECIDE BY AUG 31 2026**
   (fiction close). Policy: AI-assisted yes, AI-generated no,
   EXCEPT "when it's central to the work's premise… provided
   readers can clearly distinguish" — eligibility genuinely
   arguable (the ledger is the demarcation argument; but the
   book's subject is 1983, not AI). Upside even on a losing or
   refused entry: every entrant gets a written PW-reviewer
   Critic's Report (citable trade-adjacent coverage). If entered:
   disclose fully, let them rule.
3. **NaNoGenMo (Nov 2026)** — free, zero prestige, but the one
   community that will read the session-grain ledger with expert
   interest. Entries are generated during November, so the book
   itself can't be "entered"; frame around the documented
   process/pipeline record.

## Watch list

- **Hyperstition "Unslop" AI-fiction contest** — the most credible
  non-natty scene (2026: $10k, judges incl. Gwern Branwen); but
  short-form, zero-human-editing format = opposite pole from TBA.
  2027 edition unconfirmed; watch for a long-form/collaboration
  track.
- **ICCC 2027** (computational creativity conf.) — the
  attribution-ledger *methodology* is a genuine paper/demo;
  possibly worth more to the project's legacy than any indie
  medal. 2026 edition already ran (Coimbra, June).

## Ruled out / not worth it

- **Hoshi Shinichi Award** — verified Japanese-only despite the
  famous "non-human applicants welcome" clause. Eligible only via
  a commissioned translation; defensible only as a statement, not
  odds (13th ed.: 491/2,107 entries AI-assisted).
- **Kindle Storyteller** — requires KDP Select exclusivity
  (conflicts with the public edition); contest-level AI clause
  unverified (Amazon T&C pages 503'd). Window closes Aug 31.
- **SPSFC / Foreword INDIES / Nebula-tier** — explicit bans.
- **Eric Hoffer** — silent on AI; only after a written yes
  (Montaigne Medal would be the interesting sub-target).
- **National Indie Excellence + similar** — award-mill economics,
  no signal.
- **ELO Coover Award** — electronic literature; a print novel
  doesn't fit unless reframed around the web/process edition
  (stretch; 2027 possible). **Prix Ars Electronica** — no
  prose-fit category this cycle.
