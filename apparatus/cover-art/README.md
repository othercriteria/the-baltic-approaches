# Cover art

`muller-hall-of-antiquities.jpg` — Adam August Müller (Danish,
1811–1844), *The Hall of Antiquities at Charlottenborg Palace,
Copenhagen*, 1830. Oil on canvas. The Metropolitan Museum of
Art, New York, accession 2018.280.

Image: The Met Open Access program, **CC0** (public-domain work,
public-domain dedication on the digitization). 2889×3705 px
original-scan download. Freely redistributable; safe for this
public repo.

Selected as THE cover 2026-07-27 (DK ratification; campaign
history in planning/cover-brief.md). Effective resolution at
5.75×8.75in bleed geometry: ~423dpi. The cover build recomposes
the crop from this source (scripts/cover-art.py) — it never
scales a comp.
