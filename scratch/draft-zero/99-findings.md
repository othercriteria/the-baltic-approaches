## 0. THE HEADLINE: the 55k plan yields 30k at natural stride

Narrative total: **29,936 words** against the 55k target — chapter
actuals ran 55–75% of their targets from ch. 1 onward, uniformly,
under conscious counter-pressure from ch. 4. **The White Buffalo
number (31k) reproduced itself almost exactly, from a plan built
specifically to avoid it.** This is the strongest evidence yet
about the undershoot phenomenon, and it decomposes cleanly:

- **The model's natural chapter is ~1,200–1,800 words**: it drives
  each chapter to its payload and stops. "Done" = the argument
  landed, not the material exhausted.
- **The missing mass is SCENE, specifically dialogue-in-real-time.**
  The Goal is ~80% people talking in rooms at conversational pace;
  Draft Zero is heavily narrated summary with dialogue islands.
  Battles, conferences, execution — all compressed to chronicle.
  The prose is dense and (I think) good, but it is a NOVELLA
  register: WB's register. The Goal's fullness is not padding; it
  is *scenes allowed to run at the speed of speech*.
- **Therefore the honest 55k is not "write longer chapters."** It
  is: (a) convert narration to scene systematically (the morning
  market, the rationing protests, the capitals' pressure — all
  summarized here, all scene-able); (b) restore the cut material
  the plan already priced (division/Karup interleaves, fuller
  B-plot, second iteration cycles per station); (c) accept that
  at natural stride this story-shape is ~30k, so the 55k version
  needs BOTH the stride correction AND more story. The premise's
  "natural size 50–60k" claim survives only via (a)+(b) — it is
  not automatic. This must go to DK undecorated.

## 3. Honest failures (revision targets, or counter-evidence)

- **The competence ramp is too clean.** The confusion clause is
  strong through ch. 9 and GONE by ch. 14 — three weeks from
  wrong-maps to mastery, and Part III's staff is never wrong
  again (the model errs, but never the protagonists). The Goal
  keeps Rogo behind the reader throughout; my staff overtakes the
  reader around ch. 15. The reader-contract's "adrift/earnest"
  needs Part III failures that COST something (the draft's ch. 39-
  equivalent — success creating a new crisis — never happened;
  the win is unpunctured). This is the single biggest revision
  need and a real risk in the design, not just the prose.
- **B-plot at ~4% of words** — the friction variant is cheap but
  landed at Phoenix/Paige thinness. Marei has two scenes and one
  aphorism. Either grow it (the 55k version has room) or accept a
  thin-B book knowingly. Aakjær/Korsør (displaced carrier) got
  one beat; it wants two more.
- **Everyone speaks the same aphoristic register** by ch. 12 —
  Hvidt, Rahn, Aakjær, Zawadzki are four flavors of one voice
  (mine). Ensemble differentiation was maintained by ROLE, not by
  VOICE. A revision pass problem; the sketches matrix should test
  voice separation deliberately.
- **Windows stayed thin**: Loheide got one strong scene (ch. 9);
  Karup stayed a voice on a line; Vestergaard got function, not
  interiority. The 65k interleaf question is NOT answered by this
  draft — the windows as drafted cost little and gave little.
- **Numbers are vibes throughout** — expenditure rates, counts,
  distances all unverified against CAL benches (deliberately;
  SOC). The doctrine/hardware audit pass exists for this. Two
  flagged anachronism risks: "E45" designation in 1983 (route
  renumbering — verify), and the counter-battery-count-as-
  countdown device needs a CAL sanity check.
- **The war-origin is gestural** (correctly, per scope) and the
  cease-fire arrives by radio (per victory-scoping — but it reads
  thin; one more beat of the war ending AROUND the corps would
  earn it).
