## After chs. 1–3 (drafted 2026-07-23)

1. **The WB undershoot is live in THIS model, immediately and
   severely.** Actuals vs targets: ch1 2,349/3,000 (−22%), ch2
   1,778/2,500 (−29%), ch3 1,544/3,000 (−49%). Running total 5,671
   vs 8,500. Diagnosis: I default to single-scene chapters that
   reach their payload and stop — each chapter is "done" when its
   argument lands, not when its material is exhausted. The Goal's
   chapters carry 2–3 scenes plus connective tissue. **Correction
   applied from ch4: multi-scene chapters, and the B-plot/texture
   material gets its scene-space instead of being folded into
   summary.** If uncorrected, the 20-chapter plan yields ~35k —
   the counter-brief's §2 connective-tissue warning was, if
   anything, inverted: my failure mode is not unbudgeted
   transitions but missing MIDDLES.
2. **A′ works so far at the craft level**: ENDEX gave S1 (the
   Endstand's shine, Brauer's uncosted-forty-minutes question) and
   V1 (Rahn's "what was it for / no until") inside real scenes
   with period jeopardy ambient. The overture's jeopardy problem
   the hostile brief predicted is real but manageable — the world
   situation (autumn 1983) carries dread for free IF used as
   weather, not lecture.
3. **The mentor voice risk is the opposite of expected**: Rahn
   writes almost too well — the hazard is he becomes an oracle
   (his ENDEX scene is nearly a monologue). Jonah's mechanics need
   more REFUSAL and less aphorism. Watch in C2.
4. **Hvidt is emerging as the best staging device in the book**:
   the new-commander's elementary questions ("who owns this
   number," "two times on every report") stage the metrics
   critique diegetically without any seminar at all. The
   change-of-command device is carrying more didactic load than
   planned — possibly a whole station's worth (S1 is landing
   through him, not through a derivation scene).
5. **The B-plot friction variant works and costs almost nothing**:
   Marei exists in one phone call rendered as recollection (not a
   call scene — the DK rejection is honored by making the call
   PAST and its content the annex's failure), and "who directs the
   directed movement" doubles as A-plot. The E45/evacuation
   material is going to carry it.
6. **English-as-working-language is a gift on the page** — it
   licenses plain dialogue (nobody can be eloquent, only correct)
   and gives free characterization (accents, vocabulary). It also
   solves a real drafting problem: German/Danish characters can
   talk doctrine in English without translation-ese.
7. **Period check needed later**: KAL 007, the Bonn march, INF —
   used as ambience; verify dates/details in revision (marked
   as-drafted, not verified). The war origin remains gestural
   (deliberately — origin design is not Draft Zero's job).
