# The Mission — theater version: one-page outline
*Bake-off entry, theater altitude. Protagonist: Commander, Allied Forces Baltic Approaches, Karup. Filed 2026-07-22 to sit beside the Rendsburg G-3 and Jutland Division conceptions; DK rules.*

**Protagonist.** Lieutenant General Holger Bruhn, COMBALTAP — the Danish joint commander in the new Finderup bunker, German deputy at his shoulder, four components beneath him: two land commands, a navy, an air force. His war is a morning meeting: the daily apportionment conference, where the theater's one scarce thing — air effort — is split among counter-air, fleet strike, and the land battle, on his signature. The hostile review's sharpest finding was that Rogo is not an advisor but line management with authority inside his boundary, measured in wrong units from above. The corps version converted that authority into advocacy; the division version restored hands but lost the operational payload. Bruhn is the only man in the theater who is Rogo whole: the split is his to change any morning, and his Peach — AFNORTH at Kolsås, two capitals — measures him in sorties flown, ground held, reassurance delivered. Against the joint plans officer: a staffer at Karup only drafts the decision the theater exists to make — the incumbent's grease-pencil problem re-shipped upstairs. Against COMAIRBALTAP: the dual-hatted airman advises the split and executes it, but he is one claimant's voice; the trilemma and the Zealand garrison never cross his desk. He is this book's indispensable second lead, not its Rogo. Bruhn's arc is The Goal's: he begins believing that splitting fairly is commanding well, and learns the theater is one flow system whose claims share no denominator until he finds the goal.

**Spine.** (1) *First conference.* Three claims on the same Tornados: LANDJUT's second echelon, priced in kilometers; NAVBALTAP's invasion fleet, priced in hulls; Zealand's beach watch, priced in credibility. He splits three ways; every component's numbers improve; the theater slides. (2) *Not even his sky.* Air defense over his own German sector belongs to 2 ATAF at Mönchengladbach; promised British and American squadrons arrive on other people's schedules; AFNORTH's attention is on Norway and the Central Front. Adrift at three stars. (3) *The feint.* Cheap signals — exercise tempo, embarkation traffic — ratchet his intelligence estimate up, and his institution cannot ratchet it down; half the Danish field army sits pinned east of the Great Belt. He suspects the enemy's cheapest weapon is his own insurance ledger, and cannot prove the pin irrational — only keep finding reasons it isn't. His conference's conservatism is antagonist and safety net at once. (4) *Bottom.* He grants the corps' deep request at a compromise rate; the sorties arrive after the echelon they were for — the tax paid in days. Asked whose numbers say deep beats close, his staff produces a consumption planning factor from a manual: the split stands on models of the enemy's supply and the enemy's mind, neither his, neither provable. (5) *Jonah.* A mobilized Great Belt pilot on the mine-war staff — a man whose civilian trade is metering traffic through a strait — asks only questions: what is the theater *for?* Not ground, not force, not even the straits as such: a valve on the enemy's time, with November for an ally. One denominator — days of red's closing window — and apportionment becomes sequencing, not splitting: the fleet struck when it must sail, the deep battle massed in the echelon gap, and the sea-state day that ends the amphibious window releasing beach watch, garrison, and Tornados at once. (6) *The hour.* The sequenced weeks buy LANDJUT its counterstroke and Zealand its relief; the straits are still closed when winter ends the campaign. On paper he ceded ground and wasted sorties. The theater held, and he knows why — victory scoped to his boundary, the war unturned.

**Didactic payload.** Native here and unteachable below: apportionment itself — one scarce resource against incommensurable honest claims — is The Goal's actual subject, and only this chair meets it as decision rather than grievance; local optima versus system goal at the scale of *services*, each component's honest excellence killing the theater; the iterated daily decision at learnable tempo, the experiment cycle corps starved for; insurance sized against capability rather than intention; every ranking hostage to the enemy model beneath it. Honest loss: the ground war arrives as reports — attrition has no faces here, and deep battle is inference twice removed.

**The corps and divisions from above.** Rendsburg arrives as the daily liaison voice with a standing request for deep emphasis — right for reasons it cannot prove, wrong in the worlds where the conference's inertia protects the force from its own G-3, and unable to know which world it is in. The divisions arrive as readiness percentages and an engineer's proud obstacle belt that, visible only from this chair, is holding red close to its railheads and closing the very window the corps begs sorties to open. Every echelon's argument arrives in its own units; the theater chair's daily work is the exchange rate.

**Risk.** A three-star Rogo strains the contract hardest: a theater commander confused enough to learn risks reading as unfit rather than earnest, and "adrift" must be built from peripheral neglect against the reader's presumption that lieutenant generals get rescued. The book lives in a bunker at the far end of every report: the sensory stratum this method is certified strong at must be imported through windows, and PowerPoint-in-prose peaks exactly where conference scenes are load-bearing by construction. If the components read as positions rather than people, the ensemble collapses into a memo war among flags.
